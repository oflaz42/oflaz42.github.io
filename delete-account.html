Türkçe

Oyunun içinden silme (önerilen)

  1. Oyunu aç, ana menüde sol üstteki profil kartına dokun (Hesap ekranı).
  2. Hesabı Sil düğmesine dokun ve onaylamak için tekrar dokun.
  3. Başarı mesajı geldiğinde hesabın, kullanıcı adın ve buluttaki oyun kaydın kalıcı olarak silinmiştir. Hata mesajı gelirse silme tamamlanmamıştır; yeniden dene.

Misafir olarak oynuyorsan aynı ekranda Bulut verimi sil düğmesiyle anonim kimliğin ve buluttaki kaydın silinir. Başarılı silmeden sonra cihazdaki ilerleme korunur, yeni misafir kimliği otomatik açılmaz ve yeniden bağlanmayı seçene kadar bulut işlemleri durur.

Tekrar bağlanmayı seçersen yeni bir anonim kimlik oluşturulabilir ve yerel ilerleme yeniden yedeklenir. Reklam hizmetinin (Google AdMob) işlediği veriler Google'ın gizlilik politikasına tabidir; bu oyun içi işlem Google'ın kendi verilerini otomatik silmez.

English

Delete inside the Game (recommended)

  1. Open the Game and tap the profile card at the top left of the main menu (Account screen).
  2. Tap Delete Account and tap again to confirm.
  3. A success message confirms that your account, username and cloud save have been permanently deleted. If an error appears, deletion is incomplete; try again.

If you play as a guest, the Delete cloud data button on the same screen deletes your anonymous ID and cloud save. After successful deletion, local progress remains, a new guest ID is not automatically created, and cloud processing stops until you choose to reconnect.

If you cannot access the Game

Email oflazzmuhammed0@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request". Include your username or full player ID if known; a profile nickname alone may not identify an account. If you do not know these details, say so in your request; you do not have to reinstall the app.

Do not send your password, session codes or a copy of your identity document. Secure ownership verification using only necessary information may be requested to prevent deletion of another person's account. Your request will be addressed within 30 days; you will be informed if further verification is needed or the account cannot be identified. Once ownership is verified, deletion of the account and associated cloud save is completed and you are notified.

Data that is deleted

  • Player account and username (Unity Authentication)
  • Cloud game save (Unity Cloud Save)

Data that is kept

The deleted account's cloud game save is not retained. Account deletion does not erase local progress: reset it using Settings > Reset Progress or clear the app's data through your operating system. Choosing to reconnect may create a new anonymous ID and back up local progress again. Data processed by Google AdMob is governed by Google's privacy policy; this in-game operation does not automatically delete Google's own data.
